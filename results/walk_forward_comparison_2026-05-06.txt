  Interpretation guide
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Walk-forward = test on N non-overlapping OOS windows the strategy
    was never tuned against. n=3 with one losing window is consistent
    with normal regime variance, NOT a free pass to deploy.
  - SUPPORTIVE means the strategy survived a stricter test than single-window
    fresh OOS, but is NOT proof of forward edge. The variance is enormous.
  - REJECTED means the strategy did not survive — across multiple windows it
    failed to produce positive expected value. This is high-confidence evidence
    that the configuration has no exploitable edge.
  - This framework is designed to REJECT bad strategies efficiently, not to
    validate good ones. A SUPPORTIVE result is necessary but not sufficient.
