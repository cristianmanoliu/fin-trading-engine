many winners along with the regime-aligned losers. Specifically:
- filt1 catastrophically removes 71% of W2's profitable trades (1955→568)
- filt5/filt10 essentially leave W3 unchanged (W3 had low avg funding so few
  shorts fired during high-funding moments)
- The regime correlation at QUARTER level (r=-0.17) is real but does NOT
  translate to TRADE level — a short entered during a brief funding spike
  often still profits because it holds 60+ hours and the underlying bear
  pattern plays out regardless of entry-moment funding

Implication: do not wire this filter to live engines. The dormant filter code
in pkg/strategy/funding_filter.go remains as a primitive for future variants
that might gate by something other than current funding rate.
