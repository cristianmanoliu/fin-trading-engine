═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  DECISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Bias α — Aggregator boundary-tick:  REAL but works AGAINST the strategy.
   Fixing it INCREASES BTC PnL by +$20,245 (+10.6%). The current synthetic-tick
   aggregator was UNDER-stating Option C's edge by clipping the 5m close to the
   1m kline's LOW tick (rather than the actual minute close). With the boundary
   tick folded in, entries are at the real close → larger stopDist → more
   trades pass the stopDist > 0 filter (4 extra trades, 4 extra wins).

Bias β — Same-bar resolution:  NON-EXISTENT at target_rr=5.0.
   Across 1,345 winning trades over 5 years, ZERO 1m bars contained both stop
   and target within their range. The 5R distance is too far for any 1m bar
   to span. Pessimistic-ambiguous flag has zero effect.
   Consistent with theory: ambiguous-bar requires ~6× stop_dist range in 60s,
   which is essentially never on majors.

Bias γ — Position-size dispersion:  BENIGN.
   Stop-distance percentiles (BTC 2024-06, % of entry):
     p10=0.11%  p50=0.18%  p90=0.35%  p99=0.51%  p999=0.54%
   p99/p50 = 2.8×. No fat tail; position sizes are well-behaved.
