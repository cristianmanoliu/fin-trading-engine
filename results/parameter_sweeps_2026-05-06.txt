CROSS-SWEEP OBSERVATION:

  Both sweeps reveal the same pattern as the per-symbol selection finding:
  CHOOSING parameters by backtest performance does NOT generalize forward.
  The "best" config by 5-year-aggregate is unlikely to be the best forward.
  
  Parameter ranges that ALL produce positive train AND test results:
    max-hold ∈ [120h..504h]  (5d to 21d)
    EMA fast ∈ [5..12], slow ∈ [13..30]  (anything not extreme)
  
  Within those ranges, individual config ranking is noise. The current
  defaults (mh336, ema 9/21) sit in the middle — neither best nor worst,
  but well within the robust region.

OPERATIONAL DECISION:

  - DO NOT change deployed config based on these sweeps.
  - The 14-day max-hold and 9/21 EMA were chosen by convention; we now have
    EMPIRICAL evidence they sit in a broad robust region. That's good news.
  - Future parameter changes need stronger evidence than single-window OOS
    delta — preferably multiple non-overlapping forward periods.
