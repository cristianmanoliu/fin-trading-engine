INTERPRETATION:
  Strategy NET is NEGATIVELY correlated with funding rate (r=-0.175).
  Higher funding (bull regime, longs paying) → LOWER strategy NET.
  Lower funding (bear/neutral) → HIGHER strategy NET.

  This OVERTURNS the structural shorts-asymmetry hypothesis from the
  3-window mechanism analysis. The strategy is REGIME-CONDITIONED:
  short-side EMA crossovers follow through in bear/neutral regimes,
  whipsaw in bull regimes. Combined with the 1D long-side finding
  (longs work where shorts don't), this implies:
  - 4H shorts: works in BEAR/NEUTRAL, fails in BULL
  - 1D longs:  works in BULL, fails in BEAR (W1 +$142k vs W3 -$47k)
  - 1D BOTH:   regime-balanced (positive in all 3 windows W1/W2/W3)
