### Why it never clears

The detector Welch-tests the CUMULATIVE live sample against a FIXED 5y reference.
For a fixed systematic offset, |t| grows ~√n: loss_pnl_abs t=−4.3 at n=64 losers →
≈−6 at verdict n≈128. The offsets are structural (2026 volatility regime → notional
scaling; exit-mix; censoring), not transient startup noise. The detector's 12.1% FP
calibration resampled the backtest distribution — a null in which these structural
offsets don't exist — so its effective FP on live data is far higher (consistent
with 8/8 benign firings).
