### Kill-bar calibration → NEEDS_RECALIBRATION

5 testable criteria, B=1,000 paths, 4 scenarios (null/deg30/deg50/dead),
4 horizons (60/90/120/180d). At 90d locked acceptance bands (FP≤20%,

| Criterion | FP(null) | TP(deg50) | TP(dead) | Status |
|---|---:|---:|---:|:---:|
| KILL_PNL | 31.7% | 31.5% | 53.9% | ✗ |
| KILL_WR | 0.6% | 0.4% | 0.3% | ✗ |
| KILL_SYM | 41.4% | 41.0% | 42.7% | ✗ |
| KILL_HODL_C | 34.3% | 37.0% | 54.7% | ✗ |
| KILL_HODL_W | 21.4% | 20.1% | 39.8% | ≈ borderline |

The strategy historically has 32% of 60-day windows with negative net PnL
(thick tails from 14% breakeven WR + 6:1 RR). Single-symbol concentration
above 40% is the historical NORM, not an outlier. Killing on these
criteria would produce ~30-40% false positives under null. Internal
inconsistency surfaced: "≥150 trades AND ≥60 days" is incompatible with
the 1.18 trades/day fleet rate (60d ≈ 70 trades; needs ~127 days).

### Kill-bar recalibration → NO_KILL_BAR (statistical impossibility)

Locked rule: set each threshold at quantile of null distribution giving
target FP=10%, then verify TP. After RUN1 a methodology bug surfaced
(window-mean instead of global-mean for "dead" zeroed both mean AND
variance per-path); fixed and re-ran (RUN2 supersedes; audit trail
preserved). All 5 criteria DISCARDED:

| Criterion | New threshold | FP | TP(deg50) | TP(dead) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| KILL_PNL | < -$33,261 | 10.6% | 3.8% | 25.8% |
| KILL_SYM | > 115.7% | 10.2% | 12.2% | 13.3% |
| KILL_HODL_C | < -$74,274 | 10.2% | 3.4% | 15.9% |
| KILL_HODL_W | 2 consec. < -$35,316 | 3.1% | 0.0% | 4.9% |

For FP≤20% AND TP≥80% to BOTH hold, shift between null and alternative
distributions must be ≥1.68σ. Actual shift/σ ratio at 90d is 0.5-0.7 for
all measured statistics. **No threshold can simultaneously satisfy both
bands at 90d. Statistical impossibility, not threshold-tuning failure.**

### Drift detector calibration → DETECTOR_VIABLE

Path (1c): switch from threshold-based to distribution-based detection.
The existing `scripts/live_vs_backtest_drift.py` (commit `94bde2a`,
Welch t-tests + WR z-test, Bonferroni-corrected) was Monte-Carlo-
calibrated at 20 operating points (4 α × 5 N_LIVE).

**TP(deg30)=TP(deg50)=TP(dead)=100% at every single operating point.**
FP(null) ranges 0.2% (α=0.001 N=300) to 15.2% (α=0.05 N=50). Selected
per locked rule: α=0.05 N=50. Operationally preferred (within-N tie,
smaller α): **α=0.001 N=50** (FP=12.1%, TP=100%) — deployed.

Why distribution-based works where threshold-based fails: comparing
small live sample (N=50) to large backtest reference (N=2210) gives
asymmetric power. The reference's noise floor is essentially zero, so
any meaningful shift in live's small sample lights up across one or
more of 6 metrics under Bonferroni. Threshold-based collapses live to
a single statistic and asks if it crosses a level — fails when live's
variance is large relative to its mean shift.

### Drift detector time-to-detection → NEEDS_TUNING (too-noisy-null at daily)

Day-by-day Monte Carlo (B=1000 paths × 4 scenarios × 365 days) with
Poisson(1.18) trade arrivals at deployed α=0.001:

- **Detection speed**: median 26 days uniformly across deg30/deg50/dead.
  100% of paths fire by day 60.
- **Sequential FP under daily testing**: 28% cumulative by day 365 (most
  accrued in first 30 days at 22%). Single-shot at fixed N=50 has 12% FP;
  daily testing on accumulating sample inflates 2.3×.

Locked rule: FP(null,365d)≤20% AND median(dead)≤60d. TP band passes
decisively (26d ≪ 60d). FP band fails (28% > 20%). **NEEDS_TUNING —
too-noisy-null.**

The detector cannot discriminate degradation severity (same 26d median
across deg30/deg50/dead). The FP problem is largely a function of test
cadence, not the detector itself.

- **Single firing = investigation-grade**, not auto-kill. Cross-check

## Architectural fix — orphan-open journal-replay deployed

This morning's audit surfaced that the IMXUSDT bb20 LONG @ 0.1766 had
been orphaned since 08:00 UTC: opened by an engine instance that died
at the 08:29 restart, with the new engine carrying no in-memory
position. If price hit stop or target, no close event would be written.

**Root cause**: `Stub.position` is RAM-only with no journal-replay on
startup. Architectural since day one, surfaced today.

### Fix: `Stub.RecoverFromJournal()`

Reads current+prior month journal on engine startup, walks open/close
events, reconstructs `position` from the most-recent unclosed open.
Handles PARTIAL closes (B2 mid-R) by setting `MidRHit=true` and reducing
`RemainingFrac`. Cross-month recovery scans 2 months. Corrupt trailing
line (engine killed mid-flush) is tolerated.

Defensive guard added in `Stub.OnTick`: skip ticks predating the
position's open ts. Necessary because backfill replay covers ~96h of
pre-restart history; many backfill ticks predate any recovered
position. Normal in-flow ticks always have ts ≥ signal ts so the guard
is a no-op in steady state.

11 unit tests cover edge cases. End-to-end verified locally against
the actual orphaned IMX journal before deploy.

### Deploy outcome (2026-05-07T20:24 UTC)

All 16 engines restarted, post-deploy check HEALTHY. **7 orphan positions
recovered cleanly:**

| Symbol | Strategy | Side | Entry |
|---|---|---|---:|
| IMXUSDT | bb20 | LONG | 0.1766 (the original 12.4h orphan) |
| 1000SHIBUSDT | alt5-15-336/504 | SHORT | 0.006251 |
| BCHUSDT | alt5-15-336/504 | SHORT | 450.44 |
| XLMUSDT | alt5-15-336/504 | SHORT | 0.15868 |

Backfill replay covered 96h of post-open ticks; none of the recovered

Side benefits of the fix:
