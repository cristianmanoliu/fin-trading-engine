# Romania Crypto Tax Notes

**Last verified:** 2026-06-04 via web (DAC8/CARF guide, ANAF 2023 brochure, EY, Law 141/2025)

## Regime (2026 gains onward)

- **Classification:** "venituri din alte surse" (other income), Art. 114 Tax Code
- **Rate:** 16% flat on **net annual realized gain**
- **Base:** Law 141/2025 — changed rate from 10% to 16% for gains realized from 1 Jan 2026 onward
  - The 10% rate in the 2023 ANAF brochure applies to pre-2026 gains only — do not use it
- **Net regime:** losses offset wins within the same calendar year; net gain = sum of (sale price − acquisition cost) across all trades in the year, floored at 0
- **Unrealized gains:** not taxed until disposition
- **Declaration:** Form D212, due 25 May of the following year (e.g. 2026 gains → D212 due 25 May 2027)
- **CASS (health insurance):** stacks on top; base capped at 24 minimum wages (~126,000 RON / ~25k EUR at 2026 rates); does not apply if income is below 6 minimum wages

## How this affects the strategy

The daily digest now shows an **estimated post-tax P/L column** (16% on positive realized cohort totals, floored at 0 per cohort, unrealized excluded). This is observational only — not wired into any go/no-go or drift criterion. Kill/promote criteria remain pre-tax.

## Open questions (advisor-grade — do not resolve unilaterally)

1. **Loss carry-forward across years:** EY says NO carryover; another source cites 7-year carryover. Directly contradictory. High-stakes given strategy variance (a bad year could offset a good year if carry is allowed). Requires a Romanian tax advisor with current Art. 114 interpretation.

2. **Perp futures vs spot characterization:** All primary sources (ANAF brochure, EY brief, DAC8 guide) cover spot crypto. Perpetual futures (USDT-M) may be characterized differently — possibly as derivative income under a separate article with different rates or reporting. Unverified.

3. **Temporary exemption:** A temporary exemption/reduction was reportedly pending Constitutional Court review as of mid-2026, potentially extending a prior regime to 31 Jul 2026. Status unresolved. Do not assume it applies.

## What NOT to do

- Do not re-derive the rate from the 2023 ANAF brochure — it shows 10% which is the pre-2026 rate
- Do not treat the digest post-tax column as a decision input
- Do not file without a Romanian tax advisor given the 3 open questions above
